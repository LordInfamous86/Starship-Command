FLEET OPERATIONS / MISSION BRIEF

THE SILENT RELAY

A distress beacon has gone quiet beyond the Kestrel Belt. Command the UES Meridian, restore the relay signal, and get your crew out alive.

OP-07
PRIORITY

YOUR OBJECTIVE

Build relay link progress to 100%, then choose how to finish: repair the relay, extract its data, or withdraw with the crew.

COMMANDS

Scan advances the relay link. Repair restores hull. Stabilize restores reactor power. Each command costs one turn and consumes 5 oxygen; Scan, Repair, and Stabilize also cost power. Crew assignments are free. Advance Turn waits without issuing another command.

CREW & CONSEQUENCES

Assign specialists to improve their departments. Advance Turn consumes oxygen and can trigger crises. Each crisis pauses the bridge for a decision.

MISSION FAILURE

The run ends if time expires, hull integrity reaches zero, or crew condition reaches zero. The campaign autosaves to the local server when available, with a browser backup.
